# Everyday Italian version 2 content contract

Implementation contract for the approved zero-to-C2 plan. Preserve all v1 data and modules for legacy lessons and review. Root owns shared integration, store/model, routes, service worker and rendering. Content authors own separate new packs in `data/course-v2/`.

Each pack is `{version:2,level,title,sources:[{title,url}],units:[{id,title,description,lessons:[]}]}`. Levels are `Foundations,A1,A2,B1,B2,C1,C2`. New lesson IDs start `v2-` and remain stable; target IDs are `${lesson.id}.${targetKey}`. A lesson has `id,title,outcome,minutes,prerequisites:[lessonId],related:[{entryId,caseId?}],takeaway,targets,steps`. Related IDs must exist, but can be empty when no exact dictionary sense fits. Each lesson must be coherent and teach its own necessary language; prerequisite links support exploration rather than hard locks.

Targets: `{id,label,explanation,facets:[string],minIndependent:2,requiresProduction:false,modality:'language'|'reading'|'listening',repair:{title,body,examples:[{it,en}]}}`. Facets represent real contrasting uses of the target, not question IDs. Independent evidence must cover all facets and fresh contexts; production requirement is explicit and not silently inferred from a choice. `minIndependent` is target-specific, minimum 2, not a whole-lesson pass count. Multiple-target lessons need actual evidence for each target. Grammar targets never complete verb cases; the vocabulary drill completes words.

Steps have unique `id`, `kind` and an optional `target`. A `reserve:true` question or passage belongs to the repair/review bank and is skipped in the normal path; a reserved source is still available beside its question. Models that disclose later answers must come after those checks. They are ordered explicitly. Every lesson contains preparation, a model before its questions, supported practice, fresh independent variants for all targets, application and recap. The renderer supports:

- `kind:'words'`: `title,body?,words:[{it,en,article?,plural?,note?,entryId?}]`. Introduce the language needed for the following questions. Glosses must reflect the intended sense. Can introduce a whole phrase rather than pretending its grammar is already known.
- `kind:'words-check'`: never authored. Runtime synthesises these steps (`synthesized:true`, `format:'match'`, `title:'Le parole di oggi'`) from the lesson's first `words` step and inserts them right after it, once at least three glosses resolve to dictionary word entries (`js/learning/course-words.js`: a leading article is stripped, exact headword first, then the gloss `entryId`, then plural and feminine forms; verbs resolve but never reach a board). Ids are `${lesson.id}.words-check.${board}.${round}`. Boards, in this order: `meaning` (left: Italian headword with its article for nouns; right: the first English sense, or the lesson gloss when two words would share it), `recall` (the same pairs reversed) and `forms` (left: article; right: noun; one singular row and, when the noun has a plural, one plural row; only nouns with a known article; within a round all articles and all forms are distinct). A round holds 2 to 6 pairs; further words go to the next round. Each pair is `{left,right,entryId,skill:'meaning'|'recall'|'article'|'plural',objectiveId,contentVersion,form?,say}`; `objectiveId` is the word lesson target id for that skill (`${entryId}::lesson::meaning::meaning`, `…::meaning::recall`, `…::forms::article`, `…::forms::plural`) and `say` is the Italian spoken on a match. The step also carries `board`, `round`, `prompt` and `entryIds`. It is neither a question nor a target: `assessCourseAnswer` leaves it `ungraded`, nothing is repaired or deferred, and `advanceCourse` moves on only when every pair is matched. Each match goes through `recordCoursePairMatch` and records one event for the row with `policy:'journey-v1'`, `wordPolicy:'word-lesson-match-v1'`, `kind:'word'`, `courseLessonId`, `wordSlotId`, `entryId`, `objectiveId`/`targetId`, `contentVersion`, `chapterId:'meaning'|'forms'`, `skill`, `activityKind:'guided'`, `mode:'recognition'`, `assistance:['matching']`, `firstAttempt`, `ok`, `outcome:'correct'|'incorrect'`, `errorTags` (`matching-mismatch` on a miss), `xp:0` and `countStats:false`. Credit rule: a word is complete when, within one session, every required target of its word lesson (meaning and recall always; article and plural when the plan has them) has an ok `word-lesson-match-v1` event at the plan's content version and that word has no mismatch in the session; the finished step reports `result:{outcome:'ungraded',ok:true,board,credited,missed}` and the player marks each credited word learned (`store.markLearned`, 10 XP) unless it already was. Mismatched words are listed to look at again and earn nothing until a later replay. `lesson.wordEntryIds` (set at runtime, never authored) lists the ids the boards can credit. Sessions carrying the drill use `planVersion:3`; an older saved session starts the lesson afresh with its evidence untouched.
- `kind:'teach'`: `title,body,examples:[{it,en}],tip?,introduces:[targetId]`. One teachable contrast at a time. More than one teaching step is allowed/expected where needed.
- `kind:'question'`: `target,facet,stage:'guided'|'independent',contextKey` and `format:'choice'|'type'|'order'|'match'`, `prompt,context?,translation?,answer?,accepted?:[],options?:[],tokens?:[],pairs?:[{left,right}],explanation,hint,speak?`. `guided` shows a real hint/model cue. `context` NEVER displays the answer for independent order/translation tasks. `background?:[{it,en}]` supplies incidental language for independent work. `passageId` keeps a reading source available beside its question; `audioId` links the corresponding listening source. `supportTokens` lists forms whose lookup would disclose the target answer. `errors?:[{answer,tag,explanation}]` permits an exact reviewed error diagnosis. `type` also has `strict:true` only for tightly bounded answers; otherwise unrecognized input is ungraded. Meaning-choice answers may be English; `speak` must always be correct Italian. Matching answers are derived from pairs, not a string. At least three independent variants per target are needed for repair/review (more where facets/production require it), with actual contextual variation. Similar/identical exposed answers share `exposureGroup` if applicable.
- `kind:'passage'`: `title,it,en,mode:'read'|'listen',audioId?,task?`. Authored Italian passage/dialogue, plain text with real line breaks, natural and level appropriate. Listening audio will be bundled by root; use stable `audioId: step.id`, not a made-up URL. Text alternative always available; transcript exposure affects only listening evidence. New listening questions may additionally use `audioId` referring to this passage, `modality:'listening'`, with no answer-revealing context or translation. Listening checks require reviewed audio and separate target evidence; if unavailable they are practice, not independent listening credit.
- `kind:'portfolio'`: `title,prompt,mode:'write'|'speak'|'interact'|'mediate',model,it?,en?,rubric:[string],partnerPrompt?`. Longer/open use is ungraded, saved as local practice, with model comparison and self-review. Never claims C2 certification or automated semantic grading. Include a real Italian model and specific criteria. Speaking and interaction allow local record-and-compare, or practice without recording. Real interaction evidence requires an interlocutor and appropriate assessment.

Authored linear steps are the main path. Runtime inserts target-specific repair and a fresh supported/independent example as needed; it never advances to untaught target material. Users can pause or explicitly defer a target; deferred targets remain unfinished. At the end, unresolved targets get fresh eligible checks from that target's authored bank. No hidden quota can complete an unassessed facet, and no infinite identical retry is allowed. Exhausted variants offer teaching or save-for-later.

Pure engine modules integrated and maintained by root: `js/learning/course-v2-engine.js`, `js/learning/course-v2-state.js` and `js/learning/course-words.js` (`resolveLessonWords`, `wordsCheckSteps`, `lessonWordIds`). Expected exports from engine: `createCourseSession(lesson,{mode,objective,now})`, `compatibleCourseSession(lesson,session)`, `currentCourseStep(lesson,session)`, `advanceCourse(lesson,session,learning)`, `submitCourseAnswer(lesson,session,value,{reveal,now})` returning `{session,event?,result}`, `recordCoursePairMatch(lesson,session,{left,right,now})` returning `{session,events,complete}` for `words-check` steps, `deferCourseTarget(lesson,session)`, `courseSessionProgress(lesson,session,learning)`, `assessCourseAnswer(step,value)`. State export `courseSkill(events,now)` should accept normalized v2 event fields, produce compatible skill-state fields (`ready,remembered,due,isDue,status,sessionEvidence,unresolvedErrors`). Pure modules must not import store/model, avoiding a cycle.

Session uses `entryId:'g:'+lesson.id` (v2-prefixed ID), `objectiveIds:targets.map(t=>t.id)`, `courseV2:{version:2,planVersion:3,stepIndex,phase,history,historyCursor,draft,tokens,matched,assistance,result,...}` (`compatibleCourseSession` requires `planVersion:3`). `mode` remains lesson/review. Root will preserve arbitrary plain serializable session fields and add normalization for `policy:'grammar-v2',kind:'grammar'` events and for the drill's `policy:'journey-v1'` word events, keeping `wordPolicy`, `wordSlotId` and `courseLessonId` through normalization and merge. Events must include durable per-submission ID to prevent reload/double tap duplication; `objectiveId,entryId,sessionId,index,variantId,contextId,facet,requiredFacets,minIndependent,requiresProduction,modality,grammarPhase,assistance,firstAttempt,outcome,ok,xp,contentVersion`. Open/uncertain/portfolio responses are `outcome:'ungraded'` with zero XP and never correctness evidence. Target explanations and required facets come from authored content, not from user-controlled imported records at rendering time.

Root will integrate the v2 loader alongside the legacy registry, preserving old routes and due reviews. New course defaults to Foundations for new users, selected levels remain available, and legacy saved sessions remain resumable. Do not edit old level files or shared modules from content tasks. Do not commit or publish from worker tasks.
