# Verb content audit — 29 September 2026

All **1,185 verb entries** received an individual reading of their English gloss and complete usage paragraph. The audit combines three recorded batches: [0–399](audit-verb-usage-0-399.json), [400–799](audit-verb-usage-400-799.json), and [800–1184](audit-verb-usage-800-1184.json). The [per-verb inventory](VERB-CONTENT-AUDIT-2026-09-29.json) records each ID, infinitive, meaning, base verb, clitic, gerund, morphology exceptions, teaching policy, review provenance, restrictions, and relevant findings.

This is a usage audit and a morphology/coverage inventory. It does **not** certify every generated form or all 3,555 existing dictionary example sentences. Full source-example sets were individually inspected for 28 flagged entries. The batches specify additional fields read; all patterns were read for entries 800–1184, while the other batches used targeted pattern/example inspection. An empty findings list means no actionable issue was identified in the reviewed fields, not a claim of exhaustive linguistic validation.

## Progressive coverage and teaching

The present and imperfetto chapters teach `stare + gerundio`, using the present and imperfect of *stare* respectively. There are **38 explicitly reviewed action/weather senses**, with **84 complete present-progressive examples and 84 individually reviewed past-progressive adaptations**. The past adaptations are restricted to those authored scenes: Italian tense, time expressions, English translation and the reported clause in the formal *dire* example were checked. New arbitrary infinitives never enter these sentence frames.

For the remaining 1,147 entries, the lesson explains the construction and uses a supported meaning check. It does not invent or score an unauthored progressive construction. A successful usage check is required for chapter completion, but it is not independent whole-form mastery. This restriction is deliberately separate from a claim that the verb can never occur progressively.

The labels above are teaching policies, not mutually exclusive linguistic classes. Priority determines the recorded label; for example, a lexical clitic verb can also express a state. Finite form retrieval for a reviewed sense remains explicitly a form exercise when that person lacks an authored scene.

The shared contrast card uses these exact sentences:

| Form | Example | Intended viewpoint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Present progressive | *Ora sto mangiando una mela.* | Eating in progress now |
| Past progressive | *Quando hai chiamato, stavo mangiando.* | Eating in progress at a past moment |
| Simple imperfetto | *Da bambino mangiavo una mela ogni pomeriggio.* | A past habit |
| Passato prossimo | *Ieri ho mangiato al ristorante.* | A past eating event presented as complete |

The teaching explicitly notes that simple present and simple imperfetto can also describe ongoing actions. *Ho mangiato* does not itself assert that every piece of food was finished. The distinction concerns temporal viewpoint, not a mechanical “English -ing” translation rule. See [Treccani: stare](https://www.treccani.it/vocabolario/stare/), [Crusca: the progressive construction](https://accademiadellacrusca.it/it/consulenza/stai-leggendo-la-risposta-sulla-perifrasi-progressiva-stare--gerundio-del-verbo-stare-e-del-verbo-essere/1413), and [Treccani: syntax](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/sintassi_(Enciclopedia-dell%27Italiano)/).

The forms preserve six-person *stare* agreement and distinct formal-*Lei* evidence. The gerund has no person/gender agreement. Reflexive/pronominal examples accept both supported placements, such as *mi stavo lavando / stavo lavandomi* and *ce ne stavamo andando / stavamo andandocene*. Weather use remains third-person singular. [Treccani’s clitic guidance](https://www.treccani.it/magazine/lingua_italiana/domande_e_risposte/grammatica/grammatica_601.html) supports the alternative placements.

## Findings that affect lesson validity

The batches document corrections to misleading usage rules and retained follow-up items. Significant examples include:

- **Sense changes morphology.** *Ripartire* meaning “apportion” needs *ripartisco* and *avere*, whereas leaving again uses *riparto* and *essere*. Its source auxiliary was corrected. *Inferire* meaning “deduce” needs *inferito*; *inferto* belongs to inflicting a blow. *Riflettere* likewise needs its thinking and physical-reflection senses separated in assessment. A lemma-wide union of forms must not certify the wrong meaning. [Treccani: ripartire](https://www.treccani.it/vocabolario/ricerca/RIPARTIRE/), [inferire](https://www.treccani.it/vocabolario/inferire/), [riflettei or riflessi](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/riflettei-o-riflessi_%28La-grammatica-italiana%29/).
- **The subject is not always a person.** For the taught *prudere* sense, a body part is the subject; the person experiencing the itch is an indirect object. *Addirsi* uses third-person subjects, and *vigere* normally describes rules/customs. A generic formal-you target changes the construction. [Crusca: difficult verbs](https://accademiadellacrusca.it/it/consulenza/verbi-difficili/221), [Treccani: addirsi](https://www.treccani.it/vocabolario/addirsi/), [vigere](https://www.treccani.it/vocabolario/vigere/).
- **Rarity is not impossibility.** Source claims were corrected: *addirsi* has rare compounds with *addetto*; *delinquere* has some attested present forms; *ostare* has personal forms but lacks the usual past participle, compound tenses and passato remoto. Teaching eligibility should be separate from the conjugator’s morphological possibilities. [Treccani: delinquere](https://www.treccani.it/vocabolario/delinquere/), [ostare](https://www.treccani.it/vocabolario/ostare_%28Sinonimi-e-Contrari%29/).
- **Roles and complements carry meaning.** The audit corrected the patient/clinician reversal in *intervenire chirurgicamente*, retained physical as well as figurative *lottare*, and clarified several overbroad “always/never” rules. Lexical idioms such as *farcela*, *sentirsela* and *darsela a gambe* need their fixed clitics and meaningful complements; a bare finite form is not a substitute for teaching the idiom. [Treccani: intervenire](https://www.treccani.it/vocabolario/intervenire/), [lottare](https://www.treccani.it/vocabolario/lottare/).

The per-entry findings record whether a source correction was made or a lesson-generation restriction was requested. Follow-up recommendations must not be mistaken for completed changes; the lesson implementation and its tests establish current enforcement.

## Morphology inventory and limits

The catalogue contains **176 clitic lemmas**, **45 base-gerund forms that differ from the simple suffix rule**, and **20 entries with at least one engine-marked defective tense**. No infinitive contains spaces, although many meanings require multiword expressions. These numbers describe the current engine inventory; they do not count every linguistic exception or idiom.

The reviewed exception inventory includes *facendo, dicendo, bevendo, riempiendo*, the *-durre → -ducendo*, *-porre → -ponendo*, and *-trarre → -traendo* families, and their listed derivatives. No new gerund engine error was identified in this screen. The general teaching remains **-are → -ando; -ere/-ire → -endo**, with explicit exceptions. Rare or defective non-finite forms still require usage-sensitive handling; a constructible gerund alone cannot validate a progressive scene.

Run `node tools/audit-verb-content.mjs` after rebuilding `data/verbs.json` to refresh the inventory and source hashes. Run `node tools/test-progressive-content.mjs` for the 14 focused morphology, semantic-guard, metadata and scene checks. The inventory generator refuses duplicate review IDs and reports any missing individual usage reviews.

## Retained work

The app still has one record per infinitive, while many verbs have distinct senses with different auxiliaries, participles, subject roles or complements. Future context expansion needs explicit sense and construction tags plus individually reviewed sentences. The existing 3,555 dictionary examples remain valuable reference material; only verified target spans and compatible senses should become assessed context. The current guard is intentional: reading every usage note does not turn every source sentence into a reviewed adaptive exercise.
