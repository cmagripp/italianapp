# B1 and B2 grammar course coverage

The two packs contain 41 short lessons in 16 units. Each lesson has one narrow objective, two teaching cards with contextual Italian examples and English glosses, and at least four authored exercises. Lessons are six minutes each. Every objective uses choice, brief typing and sentence ordering; the first lesson in each unit adds a three-pair matching exercise. The question bank contains 180 exercises in total: 82 choice, 41 type, 41 order and 16 match. The order bank stores tokens in answer order so the runtime can shuffle them for display.

## B1 progression

| Unit | Lesson IDs | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tell a connected story | `b1-background-event`, `b1-earlier-past`, `b1-past-habits` | Imperfetto for background and habit; passato prossimo for bounded events; trapassato prossimo for earlier past |
| Refer back without repeating | `b1-double-pronouns`, `b1-ci-place`, `b1-ne-quantity` | Indirect + direct clitics; place `ci`; partitive and `di`-phrase `ne` |
| Join descriptions | `b1-che-cui`, `b1-relative-prepositions` | Subject/object `che`; governed preposition + `cui` |
| Express a viewpoint | `b1-opinion-doubt`, `b1-wishes-feelings`, `b1-impersonal-judgment` | Present subjunctive after opinion, doubt, wish, feeling and evaluation; infinitive with an unexpressed same subject |
| Say what depends on what | `b1-real-condition`, `b1-hypothetical-present` | Open conditions with indicative; imagined present with imperfect subjunctive + conditional |
| Pass information on | `b1-reporting-information`, `b1-indirect-questions` | `dire che`; person adjustment; embedded yes/no and information questions |
| Keep the focus on the action | `b1-impersonal-si`, `b1-passive-present` | Generic `si`; basic event passive and participle agreement |
| Link reasons and outcomes | `b1-cause-purpose`, `b1-consequence-concession`, `b1-before-after` | Cause, purpose, consequence, real concession and sequencing across clauses |

## B2 progression

| Unit | Lesson IDs | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Manage viewpoint and time | `b2-past-subjunctive`, `b2-imperfect-subjunctive`, `b2-pluperfect-subjunctive`, `b2-tense-relations` | Present, past, imperfect and pluperfect subjunctive relative to present and past viewpoints |
| Reflect on unreal outcomes | `b2-past-conditional`, `b2-unreal-past`, `b2-mixed-condition` | Compound conditional; unreal past; past condition with a present consequence |
| Retell from a later viewpoint | `b2-reported-past`, `b2-reported-questions`, `b2-reported-commands` | Tense and person shifts; time deixis; indirect questions and instructions |
| Control agency | `b2-passive-tenses`, `b2-si-passivante` | Passive across future, completed and anterior past; singular/plural agreement in `si passivante` |
| Compress and refine clauses | `b2-complex-relatives`, `b2-infinitive-clauses`, `b2-gerund-clauses` | `il quale` and `ciò che`; same-subject infinitives; present and compound gerunds |
| Build an argument | `b2-concession-contrast`, `b2-argument-links` | Formal concession with subjunctive; `nonostante` + noun; addition, inference and counterpoint |
| Put information in focus | `b2-cleft-focus`, `b2-dislocation` | `è … che` focus and left-dislocated object with a resumptive clitic |
| Read and tell distant events | `b2-passato-remoto`, `b2-remote-pluperfect` | Completed narrative events and common irregular passato remoto forms; recognition of literary trapassato remoto |

The B2 narrative lessons explicitly mark written or literary register. Passato remoto is treated as a bounded event detached from the present, rather than equated mechanically with chronological distance; spoken distribution varies by region. Trapassato remoto is introduced primarily for reading a written sequence. Similarly, the B2 `si passivante` lesson follows the B1 generic `si` lesson so that learners can see why a plural thing leads to a plural verb.

## Source and editorial notes

The level progression is aligned broadly with the [Council of Europe CEFR Companion Volume](https://rm.coe.int/cefr-companion-volume-with-new-descriptors-2020/16809ea0d4), which describes communicative and grammatical control by level but does not mandate a tense-by-level syllabus. Specific Italian constructions were checked against Treccani's [congiuntivo](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/congiuntivo_%28Enciclopedia-dell%27Italiano%29/), [concordanza dei tempi](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/concordanza-dei-tempi_%28Enciclopedia-dell%27Italiano%29/), [periodo ipotetico](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/periodo-ipotetico_%28Enciclopedia-dell%27Italiano%29/), [pronomi relativi](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/pronomi-relativi_%28Enciclopedia-dell%27Italiano%29/), [discorso indiretto](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/discorso-indiretto_%28Enciclopedia-dell%27Italiano%29/), [costruzione passiva](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/costruzione-passiva_%28Enciclopedia-dell%27Italiano%29/), [gerundio](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/gerundio_%28Enciclopedia-dell%27Italiano%29/), [frase scissa](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/frase-scissa/), [passato remoto](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/passato-remoto_%28Enciclopedia-dell%27Italiano%29/) and [trapassato remoto](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/trapassato-remoto_%28Enciclopedia-dell%27Italiano%29/).

Short answer variants are supplied where another expression is plainly defensible, for example `in cui`/`dove`, `ciò che`/`quello che` and alternative completed passives. Remote-past exercises state the literary tense in the prompt because a conversational past could otherwise be equally grammatical. All related verb IDs point to the shipped dictionary; a `caseId` is attached only where an existing verb-learning chapter is relevant.
