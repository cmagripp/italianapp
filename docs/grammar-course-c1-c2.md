# Grammar course: C1 and C2 content notes

The C1 and C2 packs are in `data/grammar-course/C1.json` and `data/grammar-course/C2.json`. Each has eight units and 20 lessons. A lesson has one focused objective, two short teaching cards, and at least four authored exercises across choice, typed transformation, and word ordering. Lessons take seven minutes at C1 and eight at C2. Writing answers are sentence-sized; none require essays or audio-only work.

| Level | Unit | Lesson coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C1 | Mood and aspect | stance in *che*-clauses; relative time in past subjunctives; epistemic future perfect |
| C1 | Clause relations | concession; cause versus purpose; prepositional relatives |
| C1 | Nonfinite forms | same-subject infinitives; simultaneous versus anterior gerunds and participles |
| C1 | Information structure | left dislocation and clitic resumption; cleft focus |
| C1 | Register and politeness | polite and reportative conditionals; impersonal versus passive *si* |
| C1 | Cohesion and reference | unambiguous antecedents; contrast versus consequence; time in indirect speech |
| C1 | Concise formal style | event nominalization; compact conditional and temporal phrases |
| C1 | Idiomatic reformulation | *fare a meno di* and *non resta che*; proportional comparison; *non tanto … quanto …* |
| C2 | Nuanced grammatical choices | evidence and mood; event boundary; graded past inference |
| C2 | Advanced hypotheticals | unreal past; mixed past/present conditions; conditions without *se* |
| C2 | Scope and unambiguous reference | competing antecedents; *il/la/i/le cui*; partial versus total negation |
| C2 | Ellipsis and implication | balanced ellipsis; implications of *smettere* and *riprendere* |
| C2 | Literary and historical forms | passato remoto in specified narrative register; postverbal subjects |
| C2 | Variation and register | spoken topicalization versus edited prose; requests suited to audience |
| C2 | Edit a complex text | logical connectors; nominalization with the agent retained; parallel complements |
| C2 | Interpret and produce | reported versus confirmed claims; short notice and request rewrites |

The progression follows the Università per Stranieri di Perugia [CELI 4 C1 competence description](https://www.unistrapg.it/sites/default/files/docs/certificazioni/celi-4-competenze-richieste.pdf) and [CELI 5 C2 competence description](https://www.unistrapg.it/sites/default/files/docs/certificazioni/celi-5-competenze-richieste.pdf). C1 emphasizes the conditional's expressive range, functional and stylistic subjunctive use, finite and nonfinite subordination, coherence, and register. C2 emphasizes secure command of contemporary grammar, varied hypothetical correlations, infinitive constructions, social and functional variation, and accurate writing for different genres.

Specific decisions were checked against primary Italian-language references: Treccani on [congiuntivo](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/congiuntivo_%28Enciclopedia-dell%27Italiano%29/), [gerundio](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/gerundio_%28Enciclopedia-dell%27Italiano%29/), [dislocazioni](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/dislocazioni_%28Enciclopedia-dell%27Italiano%29/), [frasi scisse](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/frasi-scisse_%28Enciclopedia-dell%27Italiano%29/) and [current usage](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/lingua-d-oggi_%28Enciclopedia-dell%27Italiano%29/), plus the Accademia della Crusca on the [periodo ipotetico](https://accademiadellacrusca.it/it/consulenza/periodo-ipotetico/66). Examples and exercises are newly written for Parola. When usage varies by register, prompts specify the audience or narrative setting instead of treating all alternatives as universally wrong.

Typed transformations include accepted alternatives where they preserve the stated meaning and constraint. Each pack also links relevant verb lessons; advanced tense and mood links point to an actual chapter, including *congiuntivo trapassato*, *futuro anteriore*, *condizionale passato* and *passato remoto*.
