# Course vocabulary bridge and coverage review

Independent read-only review of `course-gloss-kind.js`, the noun backfill block in `course-words.js`, and `tools/phase6-course-word-links.mjs` on 3 October 2026. Result: **bounded independent-agent pass**, with raw omissions retained visibly and broader lexical completeness unclaimed.

Every currently excluded Foundations/A1/A2 preparation gloss was read. The exclusions are named places/people or individually listed compositional constructions: clock/date expressions, prepositional phrases, ordinary adjective+noun chunks, complete requests and modal/clitic constructions. Every excluded construction's components resolve through the local sentence lookup. Unknown single words, inflected forms and unlisted lexicalized expressions remain eligible; the classifier has no general multiword exclusion rule. Riva/Rossi as explicitly fictional names cannot credit riverbank/colour homographs.

Fresh `tools/test-course-words.mjs` passes for 138 lessons, 552 synthesized boards and 1,922 pairs. Raw preparation resolution remains 74.2% Foundations, 86.8% A1 and 86.5% A2; lexical-entry resolution is 100%, 100% and 98.6%. The historical minimum floors remain 85/90/85. The unresolved A2 forms `dò`, `bellissimo`, `bellissima`, `grandissimo`, `grandissima` remain lexical and counted in that denominator. These figures are entry-link coverage, not semantic approval or proof that all words on every course passage have global dictionary entries. Later bands retain their lower reported resolution.

The forms backfill considers only a previously excluded whole noun, restores all required available article/plural rows together, checks the actual final board packing, and refuses a trial that would displace retained words. It does not drop a required slot or weaken strict completion. Existing per-pair deduplication, forced-last-pair assistance and no invented production evidence remain separately tested.
