# Independent conversation player review

The review used the actual repository, controller and mounted player with synthetic profiles in fresh browser contexts. The provider was explicitly test-only; no generated language quality or production pack readiness was inferred. The root player suite independently passed its original six Chromium checks and expanded nine WebKit checks, including immediate navigation, exact draft ancestry recovery and conflicting draft disclosure. The production provider remained absent.

The independent runner `dev/phase-1-ai-trial/player-audit.mjs` reproduced five additional defects. Raw imported correction references appeared as Italian tips despite zero independently verified summary corrections, and stayed visible after their source was edited. Cancellation cleanup from an older generation cleared a newer request’s busy state. A shared recovery journal allowed another writer’s successful save to erase the first writer’s conflicting unsaved text. Recognition recovery dropped uncertainty, transcript edits and help provenance. An existing controller continued to use a disposed provider after replacement.

The owner fixed each defect. Tips now require an exact correction in a freshly rebuilt verified summary, including rule, source turn revision, quote and replacement; the absent production rule resolver emits no tips. A generation epoch guards old cleanup and finalisation. Per-writer recovery keys retain conflicts, while whitelisted recognition metadata survives recovery and legacy recorded drafts without provenance become uncertain. Provider revisions invalidate cached leases, acquired wrappers reject replies after replacement, and stale acquisition cancels its scope without disposing a shared service.

Before reports remain in `ai-player-audit.json` and `ai-player-recovery-audit-before.json`; intermediate reports preserve the staged outcomes. The final independent [provider and recovery rerun](ai-player-provider-audit-after.json) confirms zero imported/stale tips, the newer request remaining busy, both writers’ texts still accessible, recovered uncertainty reaching generation, and one request each to the original and replacement providers. No user records were accessed. Installed device, full backup and actual speech quality gates remain separate. The player’s speech panel and setup support choices require their next implementation and measured provider before those capabilities may be described as ready.
