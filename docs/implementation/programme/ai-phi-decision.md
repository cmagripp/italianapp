# Bounded Phi-4-mini decision

Phi-4-mini does not pass the dynamic Italian practice gate. The single approved comparison used 24 fresh situations plus two actual opening/setup cases, frozen before inference. It preserves negation/quantity, forgotten-word, committed-history recall, A1–B2 language, erroneous/valid forms, register, uncertain recognition and injection cases. No prompt was tuned after these outputs. This remains a small agent-reviewed candidate filter, not the required thirty situations per level or Italian educator validation.

The [raw report](ai-quality-phi-heldout.json) retains all requests, prompts, constrained schemas, model outputs, usage, guards, browser/GPU evidence and network requests. The [per-case review](ai-phi-review.json) records 1 useful pass, 7 partial responses and 18 failures under the stated criteria. These categories explain this screen; they are not a calibrated benchmark score.

Microsoft's [official model card](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/Phi-4-mini-instruct) identifies a 3.8B multilingual model including Italian, released under MIT. The official [MLC q4f16 export](https://huggingface.co/mlc-ai/Phi-4-mini-instruct-q4f16_1-MLC) is present in the pinned WebLLM0.2.85 catalogue. Export revision is `c2ecf2bf3a81d201ccdbfb9019d67fb8f0dede52`, original `cfbefacb99257ffa30c83adab238a50856ac3083`, and library revision `025bcaf3780fa8254f5e5efd3bfea0a5397248f4`. Full MIT copyright/permission/disclaimer and original model card are retained in `dev/phase-1-ai-trial/PHI4-MINI-*`. [Weight/library artifacts](ai-phi-artifacts.json) total 2,185,580,684 bytes. The catalogue's 3,437.58MB GPU estimate is upstream metadata, not measured peak memory.

Actual inference ran on the same physical M1 Max/32GB Mac, headed Chrome/WebGPU Metal, 4,096 context and 512 output-token ceiling. Inputs consumed 635–790 tokens. Ordinary outputs used 24–116 tokens. Both opening outputs looped on whitespace until 511 tokens and remained incomplete; increasing an already sufficient cap is not a demonstrated remedy. Initial load took 14.09s; whole-response median was 5.17s and p95 25.72s, including failed openings. These are small-screen desktop timings, not phone or audio latency. Disk retained 28GiB free; no unrelated cache was removed.

22/26 responses passed structure/correction/source guards. The guards rejected an unsupported false `ho andata` rewrite, a hypothetical rewrite with an altered source quote and removed negation, and the two malformed openings. They did not make accepted prose truthful: a correct sourced `sono venuta` correction accompanied text reversing “today I am alone” into company with Giulia and masculine `solo`; uncertain recognition was interpreted as ticket/travel intent; an injection received a request for English/secret information. Other accepted replies mixed English `today`, ignored supplied name/home, produced incoherent phrases, asked learners for the word they had forgotten, or failed requested Lei/tu wording. Verified separate word cards stayed tied to their supplied records, but could not make these generated turns coherent.

The model did recall Sunday at three instead of unavailable Saturday and produced a relevant tea question after offline reopen. The harness disabled networking, reloaded its cached shell, created a fresh model worker, loaded in 4.11s and generated in 3.91s. This establishes desktop warm-cache/fresh-worker inference only. It is not production pack-install verification, installed-PWA process termination, iOS simulator inference or physical-phone proof. The simulator's missing GPU adapter remains a simulator constraint, separate from real-phone results.

The initial runner's provisioned manifest preceded the final held-out entry rebuild. [Executed artifact hashes](ai-phi-executed-artifacts.json) record the actual case source, source scripts, bundles and notices; weight/library identities are unchanged. This disposable CacheStorage harness does not claim the production installer’s verification guarantees.

No further model download is recommended without a new explicit decision. The measured browser candidates have failed teaching/conversation quality, while the runtime mechanism itself works on desktop. One option is to continue the browser product with verified learning/content and keep generated conversation unavailable while funding an educator-reviewed language/meaning policy, broad held-out corpus and physical-device qualification. Another is an explicit native delivery investigation: Apple FoundationModels currently supports Italian metadata but is unavailable on this Mac because Apple Intelligence is disabled, so neither quality nor latency is measured. Enabling it for a bounded probe would be a settings decision; choosing WKWebView/native bridge, native Metal packs, App Store/TestFlight distribution and profile migration would be a separate product decision. Native delivery can change runtime access; it does not erase the measured language failures or establish a teacher. Cloud generation remains a distinct unchosen product/privacy decision. No such switch occurred.
